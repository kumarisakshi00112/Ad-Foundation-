OUR STORY

Built from love, remembrance and a belief in education.

In memory of Mr. Abhay Deep Singh Luthra, his mother and brother started AD Foundation with a simple purpose: to make meaningful education and skill development accessible to students who may not be able to afford expensive courses.

The Foundation focuses on practical learning, communication, confidence and digital skills so students can prepare themselves for education, employment and independent growth.

OUR FOUNDER

Mrs. Rakhi Luthra

Founder, AD Foundation

Mrs. Rakhi Luthra is the founder of AD Foundation. She started this educational initiative with the vision of making quality learning and skill development accessible to students who may not be able to afford high-fee education.

Inspired by the memory of Mr. Abhay Deep Singh Luthra, she believes that every student deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and build a better future through education.

Through AD Foundation, she supports free learning opportunities in Computer Education, Communication, Mathematics, Science, AI and Graphics & Design.

THE HEART OF AD FOUNDATION

A learning legacy in the name of Abhay Deep Singh Luthra.

AD Foundation carries forward a message of compassion through education. The initiative was started by his mother and brother so that students facing financial limitations can still access meaningful learning and develop skills for their future.

Every class, every new skill and every student who gains confidence becomes part of that continuing legacy.
